## 4. 12-Buyer Discovery Interview Script (`BDB-006` — Non-Salesy Market Evidence)

Ask 12 operations/procurement contacts across mining contractors, corporates, schools, and agribusinesses (10 minutes per call):

1. *"When you last ordered staff uniforms, PPE, or branded corporate items, roughly how many units was it and what did the process look like?"*
2. *"Did you source locally in Botswana or from South Africa, and what was the actual lead time from quote to delivery?"*
3. *"What went wrong or frustrated you most — sizing, branding quality, missing compliance certificates, stock outs, or reorder delays?"*
4. *"How often do you top up new starters or seasonal replacements?"*
5. *"What payment and approval process does your finance team require?"*
6. **Close:** *"Next time you have an upcoming issue or year-end gift run, may I cost it alongside your current supplier with zero obligation so you can compare side-by-side?"*

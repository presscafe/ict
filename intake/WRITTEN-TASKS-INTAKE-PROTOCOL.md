## 1. How to Share Your Handwritten Paper Tasks (Zero Formatting Needed)

You do **not** need to type up neat tables, fix spelling, sort categories, or rewrite anything before sending your paper pages here. Send them in whichever way takes **under 60 seconds**:

1. **Phone Photos of Notebook / Loose Paper Pages (Fastest & Recommended):**
   - Lay 1–4 pages on a table near daylight or room light, snap quick photos on your phone, and attach/upload them directly into this chat.
   - Crossed-out words, arrows, margins, Setwana/English shorthand, sketches, dates, numbers, and half-formed thoughts are **100% welcome**.
2. **Quick Voice-to-Text / Raw Bullet Dump:**
   - Read your pages out loud using keyboard dictation or type raw messy lines straight into chat (or into **Tab 1: Bulk Paper Dump** in the live Studio app) — one thought per line.
3. **Interactive Pre-Triage in the Studio App (`Tab 1`):**
   - Paste raw lines into the **Paper Notes → Plan Studio**, set **"What Farouk Wants"** on any item you feel strongly about, and click **"Copy Brief for Arena"** to paste straight here.
